Pay follows hours.
Childcare follows days.

Those are two different things, and that is the whole point. Working the same hours across five short days instead of four long ones earns exactly the same and costs a full extra day of nursery. Set out the patterns worth considering, enter each nursery's rates, and compare what is actually left.

Nurseries

charge by the day or by the hour
Charging by the day means a short day costs the same as a long one — the usual arrangement, and the reason spreading hours over more days hurts. Choose by the hour only if the nursery genuinely bills the hours used. A discount counts only when the pattern meets its minimum days; set minimum days to 0 if it always applies. Tick Bills leave days for a nursery that charges for your place whether the child attends or not — holidays then cost you twice. Untick Takes Tax-Free Childcare for a nursery that isn't signed up — the 20% top-up is then worth nothing there, which is often enough to cancel out a lower headline rate.
