# Design System: A Wee Bawbee

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Clear"**

A Wee Bawbee should feel like an app Apple shipped with the iPhone. The ground is the grouped-list grey (paper) and every section sits on it as a white, softly rounded inset card (surface), its rows divided by hairlines that stop at the card's edge. The type is the platform's own: San Francisco on Apple devices, with self-hosted Inter Variable (optical sizing) everywhere else. The chrome floats. The header is a sticky bar of frosted glass, and on a phone the screens sit in a floating glass capsule tab bar inset from the screen's edges. One blue accent marks everything you can act on, and everything else is ink, muted ink and hairlines.

The only colour beyond the accent belongs to the household's six accounts. Each account keeps its own ink wherever it appears (a budget column, a transfer, an inbox row, a bank link, a forecast line), always next to its name and, where there's room, its rosette. Two marks survive from the banknote world because they identify, not decorate: the bawbee coin mark (white line work on the accent) and each account's rosette weave. The engraving, the security paper, the microtext and the denomination bands are gone.

Density is calm and generous: 44px targets, 17px titles and leads, money in tabular figures exact to the penny. Depth belongs to chrome only. Content cards are flat, and only the header, the tab bar, the sheets and the segmented control's thumb lift off the page. Motion is limited to press feedback, and press feedback is instant. Confirmed rejections: the engraved banknote ornament, warm or cream paper, decorative shadows on content, grids of boxed cards, purple gradients, emoji icons and default component-library styling.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Grouped surfaces: white inset cards (14px radius) on the system grey ground, hairline rows, no separator after the last row.
- System type (SF Pro, Inter Variable fallback) with tabular lining figures throughout.
- Frosted chrome: a sticky glass header and, on phones, a floating glass capsule tab bar.
- One blue accent for links, actions and the current place; tinted by default, filled only for the primary action.
- Six account inks, each paired with its account's name and rosette.
- Flat content; depth only for chrome, sheets and the segmented thumb.
- Dark mode is iOS dark: true black ground, #1c1c1e cards, each ink lifted in lightness on the same hue.

## Colors

An iOS palette: a cool grey ground, white cards, near-black ink, one system blue and six account inks that each belong to exactly one account.

### Primary
- **System Blue** (accent): links, the current nav item, every button's text and tint, the primary button's fill, the caret and native control accent, and the coin face. **Pressed Blue** (accent-deep) is the primary button's hover and pressed fill. **Blue Tint** (accent-tint) is the 10% wash behind tinted buttons, nav pills and the current tab. **On Accent** (on-accent) is the text on a filled primary button: white in light, black in dark, where the lifted blue is too bright for white text.
- **Coin Line** (coin-line): the white line work of the coin mark on the accent disc, in both themes.

### Secondary: the account inks
Each ink belongs to one account and never to anything else. The mapping is `AccountInk` in `frontend/src/accounts.ts`, applied with `data-ink` on the account's container, which sets the local `--note-ink`. Dormant accounts carry no ink.
- **Joint Blue** (ink-joint): Joint Billing. Deliberately the accent blue; the joint account is the household's shared account.
- **Burnt Orange** (ink-person_1): Person 1 Monzo.
- **Green** (ink-person_2): Person 2 RBS.
- **Violet** (ink-subs): Person 1 Starling, the subscriptions account. A flat system violet, never a gradient.
- **Graphite** (ink-savings): Savings.
- **Teal** (ink-invest): Monzo Investment.

### Tertiary: state
- **Green** (ok), **Amber** (warn), **Red** (danger): state text and the tone of notices. A price going up is warn; money in on a statement is ok; a negative figure is danger. A price going down is not a warning and stays in muted ink. Each is dark enough to pass 4.5:1 as text on its own 9% notice tint.
- **Badge Red** (badge): the inbox count. One red in both themes, chosen so its white count passes 4.5:1 on light and dark.

### Neutral
- **Grouped Grey** (paper): the page ground, and the ground of sheets.
- **Well Grey** (paper-sunk): the segmented control's track, hover and pressed backgrounds, and changed rows in a comparison table.
- **Card White** (surface): grouped cards and the segmented thumb.
- **Field White** (paper-field): inputs.
- **Frost** (chrome): the translucent header fill under the blur.
- **Label** (ink) and **Secondary Label** (ink-muted): text. Muted ink carries leads, metadata, bank names, dates and table heads.
- **Separator** (rule): hairlines between rows and under section heads. **Opaque Separator** (rule-strong): input borders, totals rules and the scrollbar thumb.
- **Scrim** (scrim): the dim behind an open sheet.
- **Glass** (glass, glass-edge, glass-border): the phone tab bar's translucent fill, its bright top edge and its rim.

### Dark theme
Every token has a `-dark` partner applied through `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. The ground goes to true black (paper-dark), cards to #1c1c1e, the accent lifts to a brighter blue, and each account ink lifts to a lighter tint of the same hue (orange stays orange, green stays green). Focus and the joint ink share the lifted blue (focus-dark). The coin keeps white line work on the lifted blue.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Blue means "you can act here" or "you are here". Nothing decorative is blue; no second accent exists.

**The One Ink Per Account Rule.** An account's ink appears wherever that account appears, on every screen, and no ink is ever used for anything other than its account. Do not borrow an account ink for a button, a state or a chart series of something else.

**The Ink Plus Name Rule.** Ink is never the only identifier. Every account pairs its ink with its name, and with its rosette wherever there is room.

**The Same Hue After Dark Rule.** Dark mode changes an ink's lightness, never its hue, so an account is recognisably itself in both themes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** SF Pro Display via `-apple-system` (with BlinkMacSystemFont, Inter Variable, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** SF Pro Text via `-apple-system` (with BlinkMacSystemFont, Inter Variable, system-ui, sans-serif)

Inter Variable is self-hosted with its optical-size axis (`@fontsource-variable/inter/opsz.css`), so devices without San Francisco get a close, optically sized match.

**Character:** The platform's own voice. Headings are heavy and tightly tracked like iOS large titles. Body text is plain and readable, and every figure aligns.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (750, clamp 2rem to 2.875rem, 1.05, -0.03em): the one page heading, as an iOS large title. Balanced wrap.
- **Headline** (700, 1.5rem): budget column titles (Person 1, Person 2, Joint), account heads on Regular payments, sheet titles, the clear-inbox message.
- **Section** (700, 1.25rem, -0.015em): the heading of a grouped card ("This month", "Your transfers").
- **Wordmark** (700, 1.125rem, -0.01em): "A Wee Bawbee" (or "Bawbee" below 40rem and between 52rem and 80rem) beside the coin.
- **Figure Large** (650, 1.25rem): headline amounts, totals and price changes.
- **Title** (650, 1.0625rem): an account's or item's name in a row, in its ink where it has one.
- **Lead** (400, 1.0625rem): page leads in muted ink (max 62ch) and figure-list rows.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5): default text.
- **Body Small** (400 or 600, 0.9375rem): button labels and field labels (600), section asides, ledger rows.
- **Label** (400, 0.875rem): metadata, dates, bank names, table heads, hints.
- **Caption** (400, 0.8125rem): notes under a row, "a year" amounts, balances' timestamps.
- **Tab Label** (600, 0.6875rem, +0.01em): the phone tab bar's labels under their icons.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Pence Rule.** Every amount is set with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`, set once on the root and again on `.money`), so columns of pence align.

**The No Silent Rounding Rule.** Type never shortens a figure. Money is shown in full to the penny; the only rounding shown is the household's explicit £5 ceilings.

**The 16px Field Rule.** Inputs, selects and textareas are never smaller than 16px (`font-size: max(16px, 1em)`), so iOS never zooms in on focus.

## Layout

One centred sheet up to the 72rem measure with a fluid gutter (clamp 1rem to 2.5rem), padded clear of a landscape notch with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. Spacing runs on one 8px unit with 2px, 4px, 6px and 10px steps inside dense rows. A page opens with a 48px top, the large title and a muted lead (32px top below 40rem). Grouped cards stack 24px apart, each padded 16px 20px 12px. A card nested inside a card becomes a group within it (no fill, no padding, 16px above), never a second card.

Rows inside a card are full-width lists separated by hairlines: label at left, figure right-aligned. The last row of a list has no separator, because the card's edge ends it. Collections never become grids of cards; the only grid of boxes is the childcare matrix, whose cells are selectable options.

Responsive behaviour:
- **Above 80rem:** the full wordmark, nine nav pills and Sign out share one header row.
- **52rem to 80rem:** the wordmark shortens to "Bawbee" and the pills tighten to 0.9375rem.
- **At 52rem and below:** the header keeps only the coin and wordmark. Navigation moves to the floating tab bar (five tabs: Payday, Inbox, Dashboard, Budget, More), and the page pads its foot by the dock height plus 24px plus the bottom safe area.
- **At 64rem and below:** the Budget's three side-by-side columns become one column with segmented tabs.
- **At 40rem and below:** grids collapse to name over metadata with the figure kept in its own right column, action rows use the full width under the rosette, and sheets sit at the bottom of the screen.

Touch targets are at least 44px (`--target`); compact controls (nav pills, segments) are 36px tall inside a larger hit row.

## Elevation & Depth

Content is flat. Cards are separated from the grey ground by tone alone (white on #f2f2f7, #1c1c1e on black), never by a shadow or a border. Depth is reserved for things that float above the content: the frosted header, the phone tab bar, the sheet, and the thumb of a segmented control. Focus is a 2px outline in focus blue offset 3px, never a glow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Segmented thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.12)`): the selected segment, lifted a hair off its track.
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: -20px 0 60px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.16)`; dark `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the side sheet over the scrim.
- **Glass** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--glass-edge), 0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.06), 0 12px 32px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.14)`; dark `inset 0 1px 0 var(--glass-edge), 0 12px 36px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the floating tab bar. The inset line is the bright top edge where light catches the glass; the current tab repeats it.
- **Frost** (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`): the header. The tab bar is heavier: `blur(28px) saturate(200%) brightness(1.04)`. The prefixed `-webkit-backdrop-filter` always comes first and the standard property last, because the build's minifier keeps only the last of the pair.
- **Reduced transparency:** under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` the header and the tab bar drop the blur and go solid surface.

### Named Rules
**The Chrome Floats, Content Sits Rule.** Only chrome, sheets and the segmented thumb carry depth. A card, notice, row or chart never gets a shadow.

## Shapes

Continuous, soft rounding at a few fixed sizes. Cards and notices are 14px. Buttons, inputs and segmented tracks are 10px. Nav pills and segments are 8px, sitting 2px inside their 10px track. Sheets on phones have 20px top corners. The phone tab bar is a 32px capsule whose tabs are 26px pills. Counts are full pills (999px), and status dots, the coin and the week-pattern dots are circles. Rows have no radius; they live inside the card's rounding. Hairlines are 1px.

Line work is kept to the two identity marks and the icons. Rosettes are hairline curves (0.45px, non-scaling). The icons are drawn for the app at 22px on a 24px grid with a 1.5px stroke, square caps and mitred joins, in `currentColor`.

## Components

### Header (frosted bar)
Sticky at the top over the scrolling page: chrome fill under `blur(20px) saturate(180%)`, a 1px separator beneath, and the top safe area as padding, so an installed app runs it under the status bar. At left, the coin and wordmark as the home link. On wide screens it carries the nav pills and Sign out (a 36px tinted button); on phones only the mark. When the API is unreachable, a danger line with a dot drops below the bar.

### Nav pills
36px tall, 8px radius, muted ink, weight 560. Hover (fine pointers only) fills with paper-sunk and turns the text to ink. The current screen is a tinted pill: accent text on accent-tint.

### Grouped section (card)
- **Corner Style:** 14px.
- **Background:** surface on paper.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px 20px 12px.
- A section head (Section type, with an optional muted aside right-aligned) sits on a hairline. Rows below are separated by hairlines; the last row has none. A nested section is a group inside the card, not another card.

### Rows
A row is a full-width list item inside a card: name or title at left (in its account's ink where it has one), figure right-aligned in tabular figures, metadata beneath in muted Label or Caption. Inbox items and bank links lead with the account's rosette (48px, or 32px to 40px in denser lists) and indent their actions to the text column on wide screens.

### Segmented controls
The Budget tabs, the Plan sections and the childcare measure choice are one control: a paper-sunk track at 10px radius with 2px padding, options at 8px radius and 36px tall (44px for the childcare measures) in muted ink, and the selected option on a surface thumb in ink with the thumb shadow. The Plan sections scroll sideways inside the track without scrolling the page.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), 44px tall, 16px side padding, Body Small at 600.
- **Default (tinted):** accent text on accent-tint. Hover (fine pointers only) deepens the tint to 16%; pressed to 20%.
- **Primary (filled):** on-accent text on accent; hover and pressed go to accent-deep. One per decision.
- **Quiet:** accent text on nothing; hover shows the tint. For secondary actions in a row ("Change amount", "Copy").
- **Danger:** danger text on a 10% danger tint; pressed darkens it.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity, not-allowed cursor.

### Notices
A tinted rounded panel (14px, 12px 16px padding) on a 9% mix of its tone into the surface: accent by default, or ok, warn or danger. An 8px dot in the tone leads plain words; a danger notice sets its words in danger at weight 550. Its action is an ordinary tinted button.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 44px tall, 10px radius, a 1px rule-strong border, paper-field fill, 12px side padding, at least 16px text. A money input sets a muted "£" inside the field.
- **Focus:** the global 2px focus outline, offset 3px.
- **Error / Disabled:** an invalid input takes a danger border; its message is danger text at weight 550.
- **Labels:** Body Small at 600 above the field, hints in muted Label below.

### Phone tab bar (floating glass capsule)
Below 52rem, a fixed capsule inset 12px from the sides and 8px above the bottom safe area: 6px padding, a 1px glass-border rim, a 32px radius, glass fill under `blur(28px) saturate(200%) brightness(1.04)`, a bright inset top edge and a soft drop shadow (see Elevation & Depth). Five tabs share it, each a drawn icon over a Tab Label, 52px tall with a 26px radius. The current tab is a tinted glass pill: accent text on accent-tint with its own lit top edge. Pressing a tab fills it with the tint. Its focus ring sits inside the pill (offset -4px).

### Sheets
A dialog laid over a scrim. On wide screens it is pinned to the right edge, up to 34rem wide and full height, on the paper ground with the sheet shadow. At 40rem and below it sits at the bottom, full width, up to 92% of the viewport tall, with 20px top corners. Its padding includes the safe areas, and scrolling to its end stops there (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). The head is a Headline title over a separator.

### Account rosette
Each account's mark: a rosette of phase-shifted closed curves in the account's ink, at 0.45px non-scaling hairlines, with a five-ring inner rosette. The weave per account is fixed in `marks.tsx` (Joint 9 rings / 12 lobes with a 3-lobe harmonic, Person 1 8 / 8, Person 2 10 / 10 with a 5-lobe harmonic, Subscriptions 8 / 14 with a 2-lobe harmonic, Savings 9 / 6, Investment 8 / 16 with a 4-lobe harmonic). Never reuse one account's weave for another.

### Coin mark
The bawbee: a beaded rim of 28 dots around a crowned, hatched thistle, drawn in white coin-line work on an accent disc, 40px in the header. The favicon carries the same mark with heavier (1.6px) strokes and no beads.

### Inbox count
A badge-red pill (20px tall, 999px radius, 6px side padding) with a bold 0.75rem tabular count in white, beside "Inbox" in the nav pills and on the Inbox tab.

### Ticks, tags and panels
- **Tick** (Payday's checklist): a 44px circle with a 1.5px rule-strong ring; ticked, it fills with accent and its check is on-accent.
- **Tags** (pending on a transaction, a Plan item's kind): paper-sunk pills, 0.75rem at 600 in muted ink.
- **Inline panels:** a delete confirmation is a danger-tinted rounded panel (8%) and a what-if conflict a warn-tinted one (9%). Account mapping is a rounded group with a 1px rule. Childcare patterns and the Home Assistant YAML sit in paper-sunk wells at 10px. Payday's foot and the clear-inbox message are surface cards.

### Press states and hover
Every control has a pressed state that lands with the finger (`:active`) and is instant, with no transition: tinted buttons deepen their tint, primary buttons go to accent-deep, rows and links in lists fill with paper-sunk, and the tab bar fills with the tint. The browser's tap highlight is off, controls don't wait for double-tap zoom (`touch-action: manipulation`), and long-pressing a control doesn't select its label. Hover styles exist only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so they never stick after a tap.

### Motion
None beyond press feedback. Nothing transitions or animates: figures, rows, sheets and state changes appear without motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put each section on a white inset card (14px radius, 16px 20px 12px padding) on the grey ground, with hairline rows and no separator after the last row.
- **Do** make a section inside a section a group within the card, never a second card.
- **Do** give every account its ink plus its name wherever it appears, and its rosette where there's room, through `data-ink` and the account-ink tokens. A new account needs a light and dark ink of the same hue and its own rosette weave.
- **Do** use the accent only for actions, links and the current place: tinted buttons by default, one filled primary per decision.
- **Do** set every amount with tabular lining figures, exact to the penny.
- **Do** use the system font stack (`--font-sans`, `--font-display`) and keep fields at 16px or larger.
- **Do** keep depth for chrome, sheets and the segmented thumb, and use the frost and glass tokens for floating chrome.
- **Do** give every control an instant `:active` state, and gate hover behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Do** keep targets at 44px and space on the 8px unit within the 72rem measure.
- **Do** use the drawn line icons in `icons.tsx` (1.5px stroke, `currentColor`).

### Don't:
- **Don't** bring back the engraved banknote ornament: no guilloche bands, denomination bands, microtext, security-paper ground or serif lettering. The rosettes and the coin are the only line work, and they identify.
- **Don't** use a warm, cream or beige ground.
- **Don't** put a shadow, glow or border on a content card, notice, row or chart.
- **Don't** lay sections out as a grid of boxed cards; they stack as grouped lists.
- **Don't** use an account ink for anything other than its account, or ink alone as the only identifier.
- **Don't** add a second accent colour.
- **Don't** use purple gradients, emoji icons or default component-library styling.
- **Don't** animate figures, rows, sheets or state; press feedback is the only motion, and it is instant.
